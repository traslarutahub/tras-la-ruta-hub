Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tras La Ruta Hub handles information when authorized users connect supported social-media accounts and use publishing or related platform features.

1. Information we may process

  • Basic account identity information returned by a platform after user authorization.
  • Authorization credentials required to maintain an approved connection, such as access and refresh tokens.
  • Content, captions, publishing settings and media that the user explicitly selects for a platform action.
  • Technical status information needed to confirm, reconcile or troubleshoot an authorized action.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the functionality requested by the authorized user, including account binding, content preparation, publishing, status checks and related operational workflows. Tras La Ruta Hub does not sell personal information or use connected-account data for third-party advertising.

3. Storage and security

Tras La Ruta Hub follows a local-first design. Sensitive credentials are intended to remain outside the user interface and are protected using operating-system-backed secure storage where implemented. Logs and persisted records are minimized, and secrets must not be written to ordinary logs, documentation or analytics views.

4. Sharing

Information is shared only with the platform selected by the user when necessary to perform an authorized action, or when required by law. Platform providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Operational data is retained only for as long as needed for the relevant workflow, troubleshooting, audit requirements or configured local retention policy. Users may disconnect a provider connection, revoke authorization with the provider and request deletion of locally retained connection data where applicable.

6. User control

Publishing and other external actions require the relevant platform capability and user authorization. Tras La Ruta Hub is designed to keep approval and sending/publishing as separate steps where the workflow requires it.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as Tras La Ruta Hub adds or changes provider integrations. The current version will remain available at this URL.

8. Contact

Privacy inquiries can be made through the official Tras La Ruta communication channels associated with the application.
